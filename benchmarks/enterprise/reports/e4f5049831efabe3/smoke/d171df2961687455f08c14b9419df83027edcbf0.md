## Interpretation

This baseline is deliberately `cpanel_reference=false`. It is a stable regression anchor for candidate-vs-baseline comparison and does not assert the absolute cPanel latency guardrails.

Source inspection confirmed per-row database reads and a hardcoded 50-row paginator. That debt is tracked in #1552 and remains a final Enterprise G8 blocker until fixed and rebaselined.
