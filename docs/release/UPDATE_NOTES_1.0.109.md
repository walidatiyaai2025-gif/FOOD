# FOODEX Dashboard Update 1.0.109

Date: 2026-10-25

## Scope

Dashboard/backend only. Customer, Driver and Van Android identities remain unchanged and are not rebuilt.

## Assistant behavior

- General questions that do not require live FOODEX business data can now be answered directly by the configured AI provider.
- Live sales, orders, inventory, customers, drivers, finance and other operational facts remain server-authoritative and require registered FOODEX read tools.
- Permission and scope denials remain authoritative and cannot be bypassed by the model.
- External AI planning is owned by the Agent Orchestrator; the deterministic brain remains the parser and safe fallback rather than making a second provider routing call.
- If a safe general reply arrives outside the expected planner JSON shape, FOODEX performs one bounded direct-AI repair call instead of immediately showing the generic deterministic fallback.
- The real conversation path no longer has a hidden 6s/12s provider ceiling: CPU-hosted Ollama/Qwen requests can use the bounded Agent window up to 30 seconds, while the saved provider timeout remains authoritative inside that safety ceiling.
- Direct AI replies are labeled **AI / ذكاء اصطناعي**. Replies backed by FOODEX tools/evidence remain labeled **Database / قاعدة البيانات**.

## AI follow-up suggestions

- Successful AI replies can return 2-4 contextual follow-up prompts generated by the model.
- Grounded FOODEX synthesis can also return 2-4 follow-up prompts based on available evidence or another authorized read.
- Suggestions are trimmed, deduplicated and capped at four; they do not mean an action already executed.
- The Dashboard replaces the previous prompt chips on every reply so stale suggestions are not shown.

## Safety

RBAC, tenant/store/channel scope, read-only enforcement, approval gates, provider token/latency budgets, Qwen3 `reasoning_effort: none`, secret handling and no-chain-of-thought storage/exposure remain unchanged.
