## At-rest boundary delivered in this branch

- Additive `enterprise_inventory_owner_positions` stores stock by canonical inventory ID and non-null owner key `UNKNOWN`, `FOODEX` or `SUPPLIER:<id>`. The latter must carry an existing legal supplier identifier with a foreign key to `enterprise_suppliers`, and the read boundary denies any supplier linked to a different store. Quantities, reservations, source inventory revision, provenance and distinct proposer/checker identities are explicit.
- **Upgrade:** Every existing canonical inventory row is snapshotted as `UNKNOWN`; never infer that stock is FOODEX-owned or assume supplier's legal title from catalog SKU. No physical stock, reservation, order, receipt, audit history or valuation is modified. Backfill is bounded and set-based in batches.
- `InventoryOwnerProvenanceReadService` enforces the existing WMS store, role, active warehouse and module entitlement before any ownership read. Missing partitions produce an `UNKNOWN` virtual balance rather than zero. Owner positions must match canonical quantity and reservation totals, exact inventory update revision, valid owner ID, and distinct maker/checker metadata. **However, evidence_type and evidence_reference are user-supplied strings and no immutable, independently verified title-proof authority is wired today. The service therefore refuses to certify ANY known-owner row, even if all those metadata fields are populated.** Consistent known-owner rows remain `UNVERIFIED`, inconsistent totals are `STALE`, and missing/legacy owner is `UNKNOWN`. This conservative fail-closed rule protects quote-time valuation consumers until a real verified source-document FK, audit and legally approved certificate gate are implemented. No `CERTIFIED` response is legitimate with the present read-model schema.
- PHPUnit covers migration/backfill preservation, new unclassified inventory, same-person approval denial, mixed supplier/FOODEX balances becoming stale after canonical writes and cross-store scope deny.
